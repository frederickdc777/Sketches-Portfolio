The Ten Commandments from the God of Israel Country
1. You shall have no other gods before me – Worship only God and reject false or foreign gods.
2. You shall not make or worship idols – Do not bow down to graven images or statues.
3. You shall not misuse the name of the Lord your God in vain – Treat God's name with deep respect.
4. Remember the Sabbath day, to keep it holy – Set aside a day of rest and devotion to God.
5. Honor your father and your mother – Show respect and care for your parents.
6. You shall not murder – Do not unlawfully take a human life.
7. You shall not commit adultery – Keep marital vows and respect others' marriages.
8. You shall not steal – Do not take what belongs to someone else.
9. You shall not bear false witness against your neighbor – Do not lie, especially in testimony or to harm others
10. You shall not covet – Do not intensely desire your neighbor’s house, spouse, or possessions.
